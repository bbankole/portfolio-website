In Celtic tradition, the name 'Bridgette' means strong-willed.

For the longest time, I questioned and contradicted myself. My soul. For the sake of acceptance. That is until I learned that when you come to terms with who you are, your inner animal unleashes.

Though many have voiced this philosophy in the past, the value still holds. I've tried to suppress myself in many ways, but I know my animal is a lion. Lioness, to be exact. Much like a lioness fearlessly stalks through the jungle, I strive to persevere through adversity.

In the jungle, there is little a lion fears. Why? Because lions know tough times will eventually come to pass. Growing up, I struggled with my identity. I allowed myself to be meek in the presence of others for fear that no one would accept me. I convinced myself that I would never be enough. It is clear to me now that even though I may not be first noticed or most liked, I hold something quite special and rare to find: truth. A sense of unadulterated, candid, unfiltered truth.

And, I am here to share my truth with you.
